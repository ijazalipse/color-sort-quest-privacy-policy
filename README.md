**Effective Date:** October 29, 2026  
**Last Updated:** October 29, 2026

## 1. Introduction

Welcome to Color Sort Quest (“the App”), a color-sorting puzzle game intended for children under 13 years of age.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when you use the App, including through third-party advertising services. It is intended to help parents and guardians understand the App's privacy practices.

The App is developed and published by **[Developer or Company Name]** (“we,” “our,” or “us”).

## 2. Information Stored by the App

### Game progress and preferences

The App stores game progress and preferences locally on your device. This may include completed levels, scores, and game settings.

We do not operate a cloud-based game-progress storage service as part of the functionality described in this policy.

Locally stored information may be removed when you clear the App's data, uninstall the App, or use an available reset feature. Device backups may retain information according to your device settings.

### Information you provide

The App does not require an account to play, based on its current described functionality. If you contact us for support, we may receive the information you voluntarily include in your message.

Please do not send children's personal information unless it is necessary for your request.

## 3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The App uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising technology providers may process certain information when ads are requested, displayed, or interacted with. Depending on the SDK version, configuration, and applicable requirements, this may include:

- IP address and general network information.
- Device and app information.
- Advertising-related identifiers, where permitted.
- Information about ad impressions, clicks, and performance.
- Diagnostic and fraud-prevention information.

The precise information processed depends on the advertising SDK and its configuration. We do not assume that all of these categories are collected in every session.

Because Color Sort Quest is intended for children under 13, advertising must be configured to meet applicable child-privacy and child-directed advertising requirements. We intend to use age-appropriate, non-personalized advertising and to restrict advertising content appropriately.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK provides settings for child-directed treatment and maximum ad content ratings. These settings must be correctly implemented in the App. This Privacy Policy alone does not guarantee that the advertising configuration complies with those requirements.

Information processed through the App or its advertising provider may be used, as applicable, to:

## 5. Children's Privacy

Protecting children's privacy is important to us.

The App is intended for children under 13. We do not intentionally require children to provide their names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, photographs, or other direct personal information to play the game.

We do not intentionally request precise location permission for the game's described functionality.

Third-party software, including advertising SDKs, may process certain device or advertising information as described in this policy. We will configure those services and the App to meet applicable requirements for children's services, including restrictions on identifiers, personalized advertising, and other data processing.

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children beyond what is permitted by applicable law and the privacy protections implemented in the App.

## 6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell children's personal information directly to third parties in exchange for money.
